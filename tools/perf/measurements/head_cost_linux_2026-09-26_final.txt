Separation of newly added functionality from regression in unchanged hot paths (details and code sites in
ATTRIBUTION.md):

- Construction and binding: at or below v2.1 in allocations and faster in time (direct -9.8%, unbound -0.5%,
  binding -9.3% median). The v3 regressions found here (an extra Arc per scope selection and a per-bind Arc) were
  avoidable and are corrected (bb67f19, cafe625). Direct construction keeps one shared scope snapshot Arc
  (section 4.5/16.3); v2.1 allocates an absent optional value (Arc<Option<T>>) where v3 stores None, so the
  count is equal (+8 bytes).
- Bound-head planning: one allocation fewer than v2.1 in every row and six fewer for the rate-limited head. The
  avoidable per-plan allocations of v3 (program chain vectors, survivor vectors, normalized sticky-sensitivity
  keys, the unbound-plan owner Arc, deep-copied rate schemas and IDs, multi-buffer origin normalization) are
  corrected (08d2393, f0c689e); per-request atomic ID copies are corrected (834a5a3, f0c689e). What remains new
  is required functionality: the section 12.1 declaration phase resolved against the finished head, the
  section 12.5 schema descriptor and the section 18.4 plan summary (bytes: rate-limited head +330 B).
- Planning time: show +6.6% median (+4.4% min), search +4.3% (+4.5%), protected +3.3% (-0.8%), limited -0.8%
  (-1.6%). Per-run medians of the two versions overlap in every planning row; the per-run medians of a single
  version spread by 10-15% with occasional outliers (protected v3 3789 ns, limited v2.1 5914 ns), and an earlier
  block with the same protocol (commit f0c689e, identical planning code, raw log not retained) showed the search
  row at +11.6% median, so about +/-5% is the resolution of this comparison. Deterministic metrics
  (ATTRIBUTION.md): callgrind instructions per plan are +0.2% (show) and +1.6% (search) versus v2.1 and 3.6% /
  8.8% lower for protected / limited. The show row additionally executes about 8 more atomic instructions per

  cache simulation to the symbolic patch-program evaluator (PATCH-01/03: dyn-dispatched presence and emission
  readers); this is simulator evidence, not hardware counters (none are available on this host), and it is
  recorded as partly unexplained.
- Managed execution: allocations equal after correcting a header-map copy for absent body media (d30e23d); bytes
