Probe method (unchanged, head_cost.rs): every category runs 1000 untimed warm-up operations (execution: 50), then
one counted batch of 2000 operations (execution: 200) under allocation-counter (a counting global allocator;
current thread only, where planning and the current-thread execution runtime run), then 9 timed batches
(execution: 5). "allocations/op" is the allocation COUNT per operation (realloc counts as an allocation) and
"bytes/op" the allocated BYTES per operation: both are allocation-traffic counters, not peak resident memory or
retained heap. "net allocations per batch" is allocations minus deallocations in the counted batch (negative
where inputs prepared outside the counted region are freed inside it; 0 = nothing retained). "median/min ns/op"
are over the timed batches. The counting allocator is active during the timed batches in both versions. The
execution row uses the probe's own TCP_NODELAY loopback responder and includes building the request and its
boxed IntoFuture.
