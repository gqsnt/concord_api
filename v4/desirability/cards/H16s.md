## Decision (frozen; evaluated in this order — the first that applies is the outcome)
W = correct R1.exact on the 23 witness items pooled over 5 repetitions (115 per provider, 230 pooled).
1. **LOSS** — for either provider a control/contrary item count (World Bank, PokeAPI, Frankfurter; 20 per
   provider) falls by more than 1 item-run versus the baseline arm; any non-doc change in the candidate; a gate
   failure.
2. **INCONCLUSIVE** — the exposure check fails; or the baseline arm reaches ≥ 110 of 115 for both providers
   (ceiling).
3. **WIN** — W rises for both providers; pooled rise ≥ 16; for each provider no witness SDK falls by more than
   2 item-runs.
4. **KEEP_CURRENT** — everything else (including a guard failure, a pooled rise below 16, or only one provider
   rising).
- Reader variability is reported per SDK and arm as the five per-repetition counts; a witness SDK whose arms'
  prompts are identical (if any) is excluded from W and listed.
- A WIN leads to migration (the generated-doc rules apply to all SDKs automatically; SDK-local notes for every
  custom format constant and public wrapper header), gates and CI, before ACCEPT.
