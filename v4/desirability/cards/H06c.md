## Problem
Survey §3 and the H06 migration audit: in nine SDKs a public generated control lets a caller break an
invariant the SDK itself documents (a credential stored apart from its per-credential state; a
fixture-only or loopback destination; a validated constructor bypassed by the generated `new`; a
credential bound to its deployment, account or environment). Grounds cited by the reviewer: Adyen
`lib.rs:82`, Discord `api.rs:40`, Sentry `README.md:110`, DigitalOcean `lib.rs:69`, QuickBooks
`auth.rs:33`, Datadog `credentials.rs:9`, USGS `lib.rs:29`, Salesforce `context.rs:83`, Zendesk
`lib.rs:44`.

## Comparator
Baseline: the head with H06 merged (pilot scope). Candidate: as above.

## Expected breakages (A3 §8; anything else is a kill)
- **X1 Adyen:** `Checkout::set_api_key`, `Checkout::set_policy` removed (replacement: a new client
  with the company's credentials and policy).
- **X2 Datadog:** `api::DatadogApi::new`, `set_api_key`, `set_application_key` not public
  (replacement: the validated `DatadogEvents` route).
- **X3 DigitalOcean:** `DigitalOcean::with_base_url` not public (replacement: its connect/fixture routes).
- **X4 Discord:** `api::Discord::set_token`, `set_limiter` removed (replacement: a new client per bot token).
- **X5 QuickBooks:** `QuickBooks::new`, `with_base_url` not public; `QuickBooks::for_fixture` added
  (replacements: `connect`, `for_fixture`).
- **X6 Salesforce:** `api::SalesforceApi::new` not public (replacement: `OrgContext::new`).
- **X7 Sentry:** `Sentry::set_authorization`, `set_policy`, `with_base_url` not public;
  `Sentry::for_deployment` added.
- **X8 USGS:** `Usgs::new`, `with_base_url` not public; `Usgs::for_fixture` added.
- **X9 Zendesk:** `Zendesk::with_base_url` not public; `Zendesk::for_fixture` added.
- **X10** tests, docs and examples inside those nine SDKs that called a withheld control move to the
  replacement route, each site listed (the migration review's list).

## Decision (frozen)

  non-withheld call; gates and CI pass.
