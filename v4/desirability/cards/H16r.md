## Decision (frozen)
W = correct R1.exact on the 23 witness items pooled over 5 repetitions (115 per provider, 230 pooled).
- **WIN:** W rises for both providers; pooled rise ≥ 16 (≈ 7% of 230, H16's proportion); for each provider
  no witness SDK falls by more than 2 item-runs; the 4 control/contrary items keep the baseline arm's count
  (± 1 item-run per provider); docs-only (as H16, already proven for `02bc13b6`); gates pass.
- **KEEP_CURRENT:** pooled rise < 16, or only one provider rises.
- **LOSS:** a control/contrary fall beyond ± 1 item-run per provider attributable to changed prompts; any
  non-doc change; a gate failure.
- **INCONCLUSIVE:** the exposure check fails (an arm's prompts do not contain its generated docs, or differ
  where docs are identical); the baseline arm reaches ≥ 110 of 115 for both providers (ceiling).
- Noise handling: reader variability is reported per SDK as the spread across the 5 repetitions of each arm;
  a witness SDK whose arms' prompts are identical (if any) is excluded from W and listed.
- A WIN leads to migration (the generated-doc rules apply to all SDKs automatically; SDK-local notes for every
  custom format constant and public wrapper header), gates and CI, before ACCEPT.
