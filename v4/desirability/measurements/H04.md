## Reading

- The gain is concentrated in one task: in pilot-3 all 6 net witness answers are sentry/t1 (the
  base vs appended path, D1 of the candidate: declared base, declared path and the composed
  default request stated separately, and what `with_base_url` replaces). Both providers failed it
  in every baseline session and passed it in every candidate session, in both pilots (failed in
  12 of 12 baseline answers, 0 of 12 candidate answers). The WIN meets the threshold exactly; the decision review of pilot-2 found that
  concentration does not by itself require INCONCLUSIVE under the frozen rule.
- Most other tasks were at ceiling in both arms (3/3), so this pilot cannot show an effect of the
  candidate's other changes (send-result pointer, short navigation entries, operation names,
  Accept outcomes) on them; it shows no harm either (0 witness regressions).
- Kraken and Cloudflare readers answered result/error tasks correctly in both arms at this
  materials level; the opaque-result complaint did not produce failures here.

## Limits

Model readers, not people; three repetitions per SDK, arm and provider are not independent
participants and the two-run range is not a confidence interval; worked examples are removed in
both arms (their value is unmeasured); visual layout unmeasured; the gain rests mostly on one
task.
