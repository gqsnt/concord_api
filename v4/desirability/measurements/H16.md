## Instrument finding (verdict-relevant)
The I3 packed prompts contain the `api!` declaration source and the items it names, **not the generated
rustdoc**. Between the arms, the packed `wire` prompts are **byte-identical for 8 of the 10 SDKs**
(Kubernetes, MediaWiki, Nager, ClickHouse, B2, World Bank, PokeAPI, Frankfurter; their wire-session metadata
matches, though the complete metadata of ClickHouse and B2 differs through rustdoc hashes);
only FRED (12 changed lines) and OpenAI (10) differ — their SDK-local doc comments. Readers used no tools and
ran in empty directories (rollout events). Hence:
- the candidate's main mechanism (generated docs stating the composition) was **not shown to the readers**;
- the differences on the 8 identical-prompt SDKs are reader variability on the same input, an **observed
  swing**, not a calibrated noise band: Kubernetes Codex 18 → 9 (`watch=1` placed last in every baseline
  repetition, first in every candidate repetition, identical prompts) and Claude 15 → 9 — pooled −15, more than
  the card's threshold of 10;
- on FRED and OpenAI, the two SDKs whose prompts changed (their SDK-local notes), the observed gains are
  Codex +8 and Claude +11 (pooled +19); the unchanged-prompt witnesses moved −16 pooled. These are
  **non-deciding observations**, not a causal estimate.

- Secondary diagnostic (55-SDK documentation-coverage counts before/after): **not measured**.

## Decision (h16-combined-review-sol, ACCEPT_WITH_FIXES)
**INCONCLUSIVE** — an instrument-validity failure (A1; verdict-relevant under A3): the pre-registered I3 R1
materials do not include generated rustdoc, so alternative A's generated-doc mechanism was never exposed to
the readers and the pooled +3 cannot decide its effectiveness. This neither accepts A nor closes the REQUEST
family. The review confirmed the candidate's docs are true against the emitter, core and SDK sources (four
samples; Kubernetes matches `...?sendInitialEvents=true&allowWatchBookmarks=true&watch=1`), docs-only (33
expansions, 116 rustdoc targets unchanged apart from documentation; the testserver edits remove link markup),
gate 66/66; all 324 wire answers rescored without discrepancy.

## What a valid re-pilot needs (review)
Prospectively register corrected materials that contain **each arm's generated docs** and the relevant public
wrapper notes (`tools/desirability/probes/pack.py` builds prompts from declaration source); audit actual
exposure and example leakage; freeze scoring, thresholds and noise handling; then fresh paired, interleaved
repetitions for both providers. This pilot's INCONCLUSIVE result stands.
