# H06 pilot scope — generated controls and documented invariants

Owner decision (2026-11-08): H06 candidate B (`controls { … }`) is accepted at **pilot scope**,
commit `d39ef1df` on `dz/h06-pilot`: **4 adopted, 51 not adopted**. Only Shopify, Zendesk,
Salesforce and Mastodon adopt here, within the [card's frozen E1–E6 expected breakages](../cards/H06.md#expected-breakages-a3-8-anything-else-is-a-kill).
The withheld pilot values are required and generate `set_x` only, with no `maybe_x` counterpart.

The broader migration at `74cad812` on `dz/h06` is source material for this 55-SDK audit, not
part of H06's acceptance. Its additional restrictions fall outside the frozen expected-breakage
list and are **deferred to H06c (owner decision 2026-11-08)**: seven additional adopters plus
Salesforce's `new` and Zendesk's `with_base_url`, recorded on nine rows below. The latter two
remain pilot adopters only for their E3 and E2 controls, respectively.

The other non-adoption reasons retain the full migration's audit of declarations, public
re-exports, construction/policy implementation, README or crate docs, and test call sites.
Strava's test description is corrected per migration review finding 3: its origin guard supports
non-adoption; the cited test does not exercise two servers. Paths in a row are relative to
`examples/<SDK>/`. A fixture-only evidence statement is distinct from an SDK destination promise
(USGS explicitly makes the latter).
